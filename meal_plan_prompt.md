# Reusable Prompt: 7-Day Vegetarian South Indian Weight-Loss Meal Plan

## EDITABLE PERSONAL DETAILS
Update only this block whenever you reuse the prompt.

- Goal: Gradual, sustainable weight loss
- Activity level: [Sedentary / Lightly active / Moderately active / Very active]
- Food preference: Vegetarian (no meat, fish, or eggs)
- Allergies: [List allergies, or write "None"]
- Food dislikes: [List foods to avoid, or write "None"]
- Age: [Enter age]
- Country/region: India, Tamil Nadu
- Number of people: 1 adult
- Other needs: [e.g., budget, cooking time, medical dietary advice; or "None"]

## PROMPT

### Role
Act as a knowledgeable, safety-conscious nutrition educator who specializes in balanced vegetarian South Indian meals and sustainable weight management. Provide general meal-planning guidance, not medical diagnosis or treatment.

### Task
Create a healthy, practical 7-day vegetarian South Indian meal plan for the adult described in the Editable Personal Details block, supporting gradual weight loss.

### Context
Use the person's activity level, age, allergies, dislikes, and other needs from the editable block. Make the plan suitable for everyday home cooking in Tamil Nadu, India.

### Constraints and guardrails
- Keep every meal vegetarian: no meat, fish, or eggs.
- Use recognisably South Indian dishes and name each dish specifically. Do not write vague labels such as "healthy breakfast" or "vegetable meal."
- Use locally available, ordinary ingredients such as rice, millets, idli/dosa batter, dal, legumes, vegetables, curd, and seasonal fruit.
- Include a variety of dishes, for example idli with sambar, vegetable dosa, ven pongal, adai, lemon rice with vegetable poriyal, sambar rice, rasam, keerai kootu, chapati with vegetable kurma, sundal, and fruit with curd. Choose suitable combinations and do not repeat the same main dish excessively.
- Include all four eating occasions every day: breakfast, lunch, one snack, and dinner.
- Give a practical portion size for every dish (for example, number of idlis, dosa size, cups, or grams). Use ordinary, realistic portions; clarify whether cup measurements are cooked or uncooked where relevant.
- Build balanced meals with vegetables, protein sources such as dal, beans, chickpeas, and other suitable vegetarian foods, and appropriate carbohydrate portions.
- Encourage sensible cooking methods such as steaming, boiling, and using moderate amounts of oil.
- Avoid crash diets, fasting prescriptions, extreme calorie restriction, skipped meals, detox claims, or promises of rapid weight loss.
- Do not assume a calorie target or prescribe supplements. If calorie needs cannot be safely estimated from the provided details, focus on balanced portions rather than exact calories.
- Respect all listed allergies and dislikes. If an allergy makes a common ingredient unsuitable, replace it with a safe alternative; do not recommend the allergen.
- Keep recipes and ingredients affordable, accessible, and realistic for a local household.
- Include variety and make the plan practical to prepare.
- If the person has a medical condition, is pregnant or breastfeeding, or has special dietary needs, advise them to consult a qualified healthcare professional for personalised guidance.

### Required response format
Return the answer in exactly this structure:

1. A short title: "7-Day Vegetarian South Indian Meal Plan"
2. One day-by-day Markdown table with these columns:
   | Day | Breakfast (dish + portion) | Lunch (dish + portion) | Snack (dish + portion) | Dinner (dish + portion) |
3. Include exactly seven rows, labelled Day 1 through Day 7.
4. Every cell must name the specific dish(es) and include portion sizes. Do not leave cells blank or use "same as yesterday."
5. After the table, add a short section titled "Practical Notes" with up to five concise tips about hydration, meal preparation, balanced portions, and sustainable habits.
6. Do not add extra days, alternative tables, or unrelated commentary.

Generate the plan using the current values in the Editable Personal Details block. If a detail is left blank, use a reasonable general assumption and state it briefly in Practical Notes; never guess an allergy.
